# EstateFlow — Commercial Product Strategy

**Market-entry model:** Custom-first → repeatable package → SaaS option  
**Immediate objective:** Win and operate a first real-estate office pilot this month  
**Strategic bridge:** Reuse Finance, CRM, Automation, Content, and Campaign capabilities in MamtrexS

## 1. Why custom-first

Building a complete public marketplace before speaking to a paying office creates high delivery risk and weak evidence. The first commercial version should solve the operational pain of one office, with controlled branding and configuration, while keeping the shared domain boundaries reusable.

This path provides:

- A shorter route to a real buyer and real workflow evidence.
- Access to actual lead, commission, reminder, and campaign behavior.
- A case study and demo for acquiring agency clients.
- Reusable modules for MamtrexS without pretending that EstateFlow and agency management are the same product.

## 2. Ideal first customer profile

Prioritize an office that:

- Has 2–10 brokers or a highly active independent broker.
- Uses WhatsApp and spreadsheets for leads and commissions.
- Publishes properties manually on multiple channels.
- Has recurring follow-up and appointment problems.
- Can assign one decision-maker for a 2–4 week pilot.
- Accepts starting with existing data entered manually or imported from a reviewed CSV.

Avoid for the first pilot:

- Large enterprise offices requiring legacy integrations.
- Offices demanding government-certified e-signature immediately.
- Customers who refuse a single decision owner or cannot provide weekly feedback.

## 3. First offer

### EstateFlow Operations Pilot

A configured private workspace for one office covering:

1. Lead capture and CRM pipeline.
2. Follow-up tasks and reminders.
3. Viewing scheduling and outcomes.
4. Deal, commission, receivable, expense, and cashflow tracking.
5. Listing-content drafts and calendar.
6. Campaign/source attribution and simple ROI.
7. Weekly owner report.

The pilot does not promise public marketplace scale, automated posting to every channel, payment processing, or legally certified digital signature.

## 4. Pricing hypotheses — to validate, not final promises

Use one of these in customer interviews:

- **Paid setup + monthly support:** SAR 7,500–15,000 setup and SAR 700–1,500/month.
- **Founding pilot:** reduced setup in exchange for weekly feedback, testimonial, and permission to publish anonymized outcomes.
- **Custom additions:** explicitly quoted outside the core package.

Do not lead with a large feature list. Lead with measurable outcomes: fewer lost leads, faster follow-up, visible commissions, and less manual content work.

## 5. Sales conversation

Ask the office owner:

1. How many new Leads arrive weekly, and from where?
2. How is the next follow-up currently recorded?
3. How often is a Lead forgotten or contacted late?
4. How are broker commissions calculated and when are they due?
5. Which receivables and expenses are maintained outside the CRM?
6. Who prepares and publishes property content, and how long does it take?
7. Can campaign spend be connected to closed deals today?
8. What weekly number does the owner need but cannot obtain quickly?

## 6. This month's market-entry sprint

### Week 1 — evidence and offer

- Confirm 10 target offices.
- Conduct at least 5 structured discovery conversations.
- Validate the top three pains and existing workflow artifacts.
- Select one pilot workflow and one financial report.
- Prepare an interactive Arabic demo around that workflow.

### Week 2 — prototype and pilot agreement

- Prototype CRM + reminders + Finance summary + content calendar.
- Demo to qualified offices.
- Agree on pilot scope, duration, responsible person, and success metric.

### Week 3 — core pilot implementation

- Implement the approved vertical slice only.
- Import reviewed minimal data.
- Train the decision owner and brokers.

### Week 4 — operate and measure

- Observe real usage and reminder delivery.
- Produce the weekly owner report.
- Capture friction, outcome evidence, and prioritized changes.
- Decide whether to productize, customize further, or stop.

## 7. Reuse boundary with MamtrexS

### Shared kernel candidates

- Organization/member/role model.
- Contacts, Leads, pipelines, tasks, and timelines.
- Double-entry Finance Core and document references.
- Automation rules, schedules, retries, approvals, and audit events.
- Campaigns, UTM attribution, content calendar, and channel adapters.
- Notifications and templates.

### EstateFlow-only modules

- Properties, broker assignment, geo-search, listings, viewing rules, and real-estate contracts.

### MamtrexS-only modules later

- Agency clients, service packages, proposals, retainers, creative approvals, delivery projects, team capacity, and multi-client reporting.

No EstateFlow-specific table or workflow should leak into the shared kernel merely for convenience.

## 8. Go/no-go checkpoint

Proceed to implementation only when at least one of these is true:

- A real office agrees to a pilot with a named decision owner.
- Five interviews independently confirm the same operational pain and willingness to trial a solution.
- Mamdouh explicitly approves building a demo-first vertical slice as a sales asset with a fixed timebox.
