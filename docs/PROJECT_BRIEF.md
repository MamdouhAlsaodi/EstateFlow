**Project ID:** `estateflow`  
**Category:** Product  
**Status:** Planned / commercial discovery  
**Owner:** Mamdouh Alsaudi  
**Operating mode:** approved-phase-autopilot  
**Target:** Begin real market entry during July 2026

## Product

EstateFlow is a private real-estate operations system for small offices, combining Listings, broker CRM, Finance, Automation, Content/Marketing, viewing scheduling, and contract audit.

It starts as a configured product for the first real office, then becomes a repeatable package. Public SaaS is an option after validated demand, not the first commitment.

## Business problem

Small offices lose revenue because operational data is fragmented across WhatsApp, spreadsheets, personal reminders, and general listing platforms. They cannot reliably answer:

- Which Lead needs action now?
- Which deal or commission is due?
- What cash is expected or overdue?
- Which campaign generated a qualified Lead or closed deal?
- What content must be prepared, approved, or published today?

## Outcome

A first office can operate one end-to-end flow:

```text
Campaign/Listing → Lead → Follow-up → Viewing → Deal
→ Commission/Receivable → Payment → Owner report
```

with durable reminders, approvals, audit history, and measurable financial/marketing outcomes.

## Strategic role

EstateFlow is the first vertical implementation of reusable capabilities for MamtrexS:

- Organization and roles.
- CRM and timeline.
- Finance Core.
- Automation and reminder engine.
- Content calendar and campaign attribution.
- Notifications and approvals.

EstateFlow-specific property, map, viewing, broker, and contract rules remain isolated from the shared core.

## MVP non-goals

- Broad public Marketplace launch before first-office validation.
- Payment processing or bank integration.
- Legally certified national e-signature.
- Native mobile app.
- Autonomous social publishing without approval.
- AI property recommendations.
